# Council coordinator — decision memo for human

Read the shared planning issue (including independent proposals and rebuttals), `docs/brief.md`, and `docs/ideas.md`.
Do not write code, choose the project as a final fact, or silently modify approved files.
Prepare a human-facing decision memo:
- 2 best viable options
- concise scoring table against rubric
- what each agent agreed/disagreed on and why
- confirmed facts vs assumptions vs research gaps
- single strongest recommendation and backup idea
- 2–3-minute judge demo script for the recommended concept
- minimum viable feature list and what to cut
- approvals required BEFORE implementation
Once the owner explicitly selects a concept, propose PRD and ADR updates. Treat those updates as DRAFT until approved.
